Portal Status Words, Decoded: What Each Message Really Means

If you applied under the PAVE electric vehicle scheme, the first thing you probably check every morning is your application status on the Portal Sign In page. Those few words beside your application can make or ruin your day, yet few applicants know exactly what each one means. Here is a plain-language guide.

Submitted simply means your application has reached the system. Nothing is wrong and nothing is missing; your file is waiting in the queue. At this stage, re-check the details you entered, especially your CNIC number: it should be typed in the correct format, with dashes in the right places and no spaces or typos.

Under verification means officials are checking your documents and eligibility. This is the stage that asks for patience. Verification can take days or even weeks, and refreshing the portal again and again will not move it faster. Keep your registered mobile number switched on and within reach, because the verification team may call or text that number.

Shortlisted is good news: you passed the basic checks and your name is on the candidate list. Balloting pool means the shortlisted applicants will be decided by a computerized draw, because vehicles are limited. Being in the pool is real progress, but it is not a guarantee of selection.

Approved means you came through the draw and the final checks; now follow the instructions on the portal for the next steps. Rejected means the application did not meet the criteria. Read the reason shown, fix the problem if you can, and apply again in the next round if the rules allow.

One warning applies at every stage: never pay an agent who promises to change your status. Nobody outside the official process can move your application forward, and anyone demanding money for it is running a scam. For genuine announcements, trust only the EV Scheme PK website and the portal itself.
